What it is

A daily digest of EU foreign-policy, Middle East, and sanctions developments, built for a Swiss-confederation reader — a SECO/EDA lens on EU affairs. Each run polls EU institutions (EEAS, the Council of the EU, the Commission, Parliament committees and delegations), Brussels and Swiss press, international press, and think tanks, then writes a dated markdown digest with a top-of-page synthesis headline.

How it works

Items are filtered by topic and flagged for Swiss relevance — mentions of Switzerland, SECO, FDFA/EDA, neutrality, or any sanctions item, since SECO routinely rules on alignment with new EU restrictive measures. A GitHub Actions workflow runs it daily at a deliberately off-the-hour 04:37 UTC — scheduled runs queue on shared runners and the :00 slots were costing hours — and commits a static site to docs/, deduplicating against a 90-day SQLite store.

Decisions & trade-offs

The project's own notes are candid about what isn't wired up yet: Swiss federal sites (admin.ch, SECO) hydrate their listings via JS the scraper can't yet reach, Euractiv sits behind a bot-fight challenge and was disabled rather than fought, and the topic classifier is keyword-based, not semantic — deliberate scope cuts, covered meanwhile by NZZ, Tagi, SRF, and Le Temps carrying the same announcements.
